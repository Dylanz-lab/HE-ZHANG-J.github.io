GOOD QUESTIONS

Know what this can—and can’t—tell you.

VeriScope is designed around public-source context, not hidden surveillance or a definitive identity judgment.

Can this prove a dating profile is fake?

No. It can surface public photo appearances and inconsistencies that may deserve follow-up. It does not prove who controls an account.

Can it tell me whether someone is cheating?

No. A photo appearing on another public page cannot establish infidelity or intent. At most, it can give you a public clue to verify yourself.

Can I upload a real photo right now?

Not in this validation version. The current experience uses fictional artwork to demonstrate the report and measure demand before live search infrastructure is built.

What information does the page measure?

Only after you opt in, Google Analytics measures page interactions, coarse country, device, and traffic source. It does not receive email addresses, photos, filenames, or face data.
