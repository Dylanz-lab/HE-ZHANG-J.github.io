A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.

Know exactly what you’re trying.

Can I search my own photo right now?

Not yet. This page is an interactive demonstration. Every photo, name and source in the report is part of a fictional sample, not a live search result.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

There is no photo-upload field in this version. The demo uses our own fictional illustration, so you do not need to share a photo to explore it.

Does a match prove who someone is?

No. Reused photos, nicknames and lookalikes can be misleading. This concept is about source context and open questions—not verified identities, background checks or judgments about trustworthiness.

What happens when I try the launch form?

In preview mode, the form is a local interaction demo: it does not send or save an email. When a real signup service is connected, the form will clearly say that it saves your email, show the data notice, and confirm success only after the server accepts it.

What information does this page measure?

The public preview has no third-party counter or analytics tracker. Its event inspector is local to the current tab. A connected backend can optionally record usage after consent; photos and email addresses are never placed in analytics events.
